<objective>
Re-pin the conformance suite to whatever is newest, re-measure once, and report the bump's delta
**separately** from the fixes' delta (D-14).

Purpose: D-14 repeats 118.1's D-08 verbatim. Fix against the referee that produced 118.1's closing
numbers so the delta is attributable to these two changes alone (plan 11 did that at the HELD pin); then
bump to whatever is newest, re-measure once, and report the bump's delta separately. **Two clean numbers,
not one confounded one.** 118.1's own bump delta was recorded as nil, which is what made plan 11's
comparison like-for-like in the first place.

**The tension D-16 names, and how this plan sequences around it.** The re-pin is the final task, and a
newer suite can introduce failures that red a gate hardened to `exit 0`. Plan 11 hardened against the
HELD-pin measurement. This plan's re-measurement is therefore a **gate on the gate**: if the bump reds
it, that is a reportable finding, not a reason to weaken D-16. The refuted
`ServerAcceptsWhitespaceHeaderValue` flake is the other live risk — if a future pin SCORES it, `exit 0`
becomes unattainable for a suite-side reason. Record that exposure rather than pre-emptively softening
the gate.

Output: a new pin, a second measurement, and a delta table that does not mix the two causes.
</objective>
