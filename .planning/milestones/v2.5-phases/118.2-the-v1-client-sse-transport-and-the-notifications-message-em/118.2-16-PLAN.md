<objective>
Make the closure's scope boundary auditable: record every finding it declines to fix, and answer the
api-coverage detector honestly.

Purpose: this gap closure fixes four of the thirteen findings in `118.2-REVIEW.md`. The other nine —
four Warnings and five Info items — are real, were confirmed by a reviewer, and are being left. The
difference between a scoped closure and a quiet drop is whether the leftovers are written down with
their evidence and their reason. `118.2-VERIFICATION.md` explicitly praised this phase for
disclosing its known-open items rather than sweeping them, and for treating CR-01/CR-02 as a
different category precisely BECAUSE they appeared in no such record. That standard applies to this
closure too.

One item needs saying twice. **WR-06 must NOT be fixed here.** It concerns `logging/setLevel`
reporting success while discarding the level on stateless deployments and inside JSON-RPC batches —
CONF-10 territory that plans 07, 08 and 13 already argued to a booked conclusion, with two
`deferred-items.md` verdicts and two WINDOWS.md entries behind it. Reopening it in a transport
closure would relitigate a settled requirement with none of that context loaded.

This plan touches no source file, so it runs in parallel with plan 17 on wave 10 with zero file
overlap. It is sequenced AFTER plan 15 for one reason only: plan 15 also appends one entry to
`deferred-items.md` (the per-id response-routing follow-up its discard-on-mismatch decision
generates), and two writers on the same append-only file must not run concurrently.

Output: an appended appendix in `deferred-items.md` covering all nine declined findings plus the one
follow-up this closure itself generates, and `COVERAGE.md`.

**Locked decisions this plan preserves rather than revisits.** WR-06 sits squarely on top of D-10
(filter on both eras), D-11 (the v1 level lives in the per-session `V1State` map), D-12 (default
level `info`) and D-13 (both dispatch roots agree about `logging/setLevel`) — four decisions the
developer locked in `118.2-CONTEXT.md` and plans 07, 08 and 13 implemented and argued to a booked
conclusion. Recording WR-06 rather than fixing it is what keeps those four intact. The appendix
itself is written under D-17, which asks that this phase's record-keeping not widen the project's
existing traceability warning — hence a plain appendix in an existing file rather than any new
identifier.
</objective>
