The spec type, `schema/vendored/core-2026-07-28/schema.ts:678-696`:
`DiscoverResult extends CacheableResult` with REQUIRED `supportedVersions: string[]`, `capabilities`,
plus `resultType`, `ttlMs`, `cacheScope` from `CacheableResult`, and OPTIONAL `instructions`, `_meta`.
There is NO `protocolVersion` and NO top-level `serverInfo` in the spec type — pmcp's
`ServerDiscoverResult` carries both. The schema does not set `additionalProperties: false`, so the
extras are tolerated, and REMOVING them would be `struct_field_missing`, a MAJOR lint. Leave them;
add the missing field only.

Today's type — `src/types/protocol/mod.rs:626-702`, `pub struct ServerDiscoverResult`, already
`#[non_exhaustive]` with `#[serde(rename_all = "camelCase")]`. Its `ttl_ms` field at `:686` is the
in-repo precedent for a free field addition and its rustdoc already states the argument verbatim:
"Adding this field is additive rather than a major bump because this struct is `#[non_exhaustive]`,
so `cargo semver-checks`' `constructible_struct_adds_field` does not fire." Copy that sentence into
the new field's doc.

The projection to thread through — `src/server/core.rs:1293-1305`,
`pub(crate) fn discover_result_from_capabilities(capabilities, info, negotiated_version) -> ServerDiscoverResult`.
Its two callers: `build_discover_response` (`src/server/core.rs:1944-1992`), which today takes only
`id`, `capabilities`, `info` and `protocol_context`; and one hop up
`Server::handle_discover` (`src/server/mod.rs:1502-1513`), a thin delegate.

The single accept-list source, which must NOT be duplicated:
`ServerCore::supported_protocol_versions` (`src/server/core.rs:503-505`) /
`Server::supported_protocol_versions` (`src/server/mod.rs:1437`).

The correlation the suite requires, from `dist/index.js` offset 370104: `ServerUnsupportedVersionError`
asserts `error.data.supported` is a NON-EMPTY array every element of which appears in the discover
result's `supportedVersions`, and that `error.data.requested` echoes the requested version.
