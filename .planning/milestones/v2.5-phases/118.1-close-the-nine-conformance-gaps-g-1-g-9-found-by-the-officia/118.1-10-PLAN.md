<objective>
Build the server-to-client channel the StreamableHTTP transport does not have: own a
`ServerRequestDispatcher` on `ServerState` (outside `Mutex<Server>`), drain its outbound requests
into the originating session's SSE stream, and route an inbound JSON-RPC response POST to it BEFORE
any code takes the server mutex.

Purpose: this is not a wiring change. `dispatch_request_or_retire` holds
`state.server.lock().await` for the ENTIRE duration of the tool handler, so a handler awaiting
`peer.sample()` holds it while waiting for the client's answer — and that answer arrives as a POST
whose first three stages (`resolve_v2_gate` taking the lock twice, then `extract_and_validate_auth`
taking it again) all block on the same mutex. That is a guaranteed deadlock, and it is also a
denial-of-service control: one malicious tool call would otherwise take the whole transport offline.

Output: a new `peer_channel` submodule holding `SessionPeerHandle` and the outbound drain,
correlation-id OWNERSHIP inside `ServerRequestDispatcher`, a `pub(crate)` `TransportBackchannel`
carrier on the already-threaded `ProtocolContext`, the early inbound classification on BOTH
entrypoints, and a timeout-bounded fence whose most important test is the concurrency guard.

revision mandated an `mpsc::channel::<(String, ServerRequest)>` whose drain snippet used a
`session_id` that was not in the tuple, and told plan 11 to inject a peer at a call site with no
peer parameter. The seam is now fully specified in `<interfaces>`. Build it as written.

## THE MANDATED SEAM (do not redesign — cross-AI review rejected the previous open-ended version)

The previous revision of this plan asked the executor to choose between two session-binding shapes
and to inject the peer "at the HTTP dispatch site". Two independent source-reading reviewers proved
that unimplementable, and they were right. The facts, all verified at source:

- `dispatch_request_or_retire` (`src/server/streamable_http_server.rs:2976-3005`) calls
  `server.handle_request_with_context(id, request, auth_context, protocol_context)`. That signature
  (`src/server/mod.rs:1634-1640`) has FOUR parameters and no peer seam.
- `RequestHandlerExtra` is constructed deep inside the dispatchers —
  `Server::call_tool_with_context` at `src/server/mod.rs:2084` and `ServerCore::handle_call_tool` at
  `src/server/core.rs:638` — and the peer is applied by `attach_peer`
  (`src/server/mod.rs:1208-1217`, `src/server/core.rs:532-538`) from the SINGLE global
  `self.peer_handle` field. A transport cannot reach that construction.
- `ServerRequestDispatcher::dispatch` (`src/server/server_request_dispatcher.rs:109-157`) mints
  `"dispatch-{N}"` from a static `AtomicU64` and pushes `(correlation_id, request)` onto
  `mpsc::Sender<(String, ServerRequest)>`. Neither the tuple nor the `pending` map carries any
  session identity, so the drain snippet the previous revision wrote —
  `route_to_session_stream(&state.v1, session_id, message)` — had no `session_id` to pass.

The seam this plan builds instead, in three additive pieces. All three are `pub(crate)` or behind
`pub(crate) mod` declarations (`src/server/mod.rs:92` `pub(crate) mod peer_impl`, `:115`
`pub(crate) mod server_request_dispatcher`), so NONE of it enlarges the public API and none of it
adds to the D-15 semver delta:

**(1) Correlation-id ownership, inside the dispatcher.** `ServerRequestDispatcher` gains a private
`owners: Arc<RwLock<HashMap<String, String>>>` mapping correlation id to an opaque owner token, plus
`pub(crate) async fn dispatch_owned(&self, owner: &str, request: ServerRequest) -> Result<Value>`
and `pub(crate) async fn owner_of(&self, correlation_id: &str) -> Option<String>`. The channel type
stays `mpsc::Sender<(String, ServerRequest)>` EXACTLY as it is, so `Server::run()`
(`src/server/mod.rs:1160-1174`), `new_with_channel` and `spawn_server_request_drain` (`:209-231`)
compile untouched and the in-process path is byte-identical.

**(2) A request-scoped carrier on a value that is ALREADY threaded end to end.** `ProtocolContext`
(`src/types/protocol/context.rs:107`) is already resolved once at HTTP ingress, passed through
`handle_request_with_context`, and moved into `RequestHandlerExtra` via `.with_protocol_context(...)`
at BOTH `src/server/mod.rs:2084` and `src/server/core.rs:638`. It is `#[non_exhaustive]`
(`src/types/protocol/context.rs:106`) and already carries two `pub(crate)` fields (`mrtr`,
`mrtr_verified`) precisely so internal plumbing can ride it without a public-API change. The
backchannel becomes a third such field. Adding a `pub(crate)` field to a `#[non_exhaustive]` public
struct is semver-MINOR.

**(3) `attach_peer` prefers the request-scoped peer over the global one.** Both `attach_peer` impls
receive `extra` AFTER `.with_protocol_context(protocol_context)` has already been applied (read the
builder chains at `src/server/mod.rs:2084-2101` and `src/server/core.rs:638-651` — `attach_peer`
wraps the whole chain), and `RequestHandlerExtra.protocol_context` is a `pub` field
(`src/server/cancellation.rs:231`). So `attach_peer` can read the backchannel straight off `extra`
with NO signature change at any call site. Plan 11 performs that read; this plan builds pieces (1)
and (2) and the transport-side construction.

The exact types to declare in `src/types/protocol/context.rs`, all `#[cfg(not(target_arch = "wasm32"))]`
because `src/shared/peer.rs` carries a module-level `#![cfg(not(target_arch = "wasm32"))]` at `:25`:
