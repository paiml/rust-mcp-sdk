<objective>
Give the binding re-verification procedure a defined answer for the outcome it never planned
for — the `schema/2026-07-28` directory still not existing — record that answer as a maintainer
decision, and correct the trigger the procedure waits on.

Purpose: `113-SPEC-RECHECK.md` § Re-verification obligation is binding and its step 4 offers
exactly two landing states, `PUBLISHED-CONFIRMED` or `PUBLISHED-DRIFT`. Both presuppose
publication. The 2026-07-26 addendum found the directory on no ref at all — not `main`, not
the `2026-07-28-RC` tag (a lightweight tag pointing at a commit from 2026-05-29, roughly seven
weeks OLDER than the draft commit this phase verified against), not the release-tracking
branch — and no in-flight change that would create it. Eleven requirements sit at `[~]` waiting
on a gate that may simply never clear, and "the gate clears on 2026-07-28" is being treated as
a scheduled certainty. It is not one. The phase needs the third branch written down BEFORE the
date, so the re-run has somewhere to land.

The trigger is also stated wrongly, and that compounds the problem. The procedure says to re-run
"on or after 2026-07-28", but the RC announcement says plainly that nothing breaks on July 28 —
it "is merely the date when the normative text is published" — and the June 29 SDK-betas post
still speaks of a time "before the new specification is locked". Waiting for a DATE is not the
same as waiting for the ARTIFACT. This plan restates the trigger as the condition it always
meant: a versioned schema directory exists.

Two further findings sharpen what the maintainer is actually choosing between, and both point
the same way. The RC tag is a STRICT ANCESTOR of this phase's pin — 236 commits behind, with our
pinned `schema.ts` and `schema.json` sha256-identical to `main` HEAD, i.e. zero schema drift
since 2026-07-16 — so the RC cannot discharge a re-verification and pmcp's baseline is the most
current source that exists. And the three constants under exception were RENUMBERED after that
"locked" RC, from `-32001`/`-32003`/`-32004` to `-32020`/`-32021`/`-32022`, where the RC values
would have collided head-on with pmcp's own `AUTHENTICATION_REQUIRED = -32003` and
`PERMISSION_DENIED = -32004`. The risk the exception guards against is realized history. That
strengthens the case for keeping the gate; it is not an argument for dissolving it, and the
brief must present it as evidence rather than as advocacy.

This plan does NOT decide the policy. It assembles the evidence, puts the options to the
maintainer, and records what they choose.

Output: a decision brief, a maintainer decision at a blocking checkpoint, and a
`## Third Outcome Policy` section in `113-SPEC-RECHECK.md` recorded in the same
named-person/date/rule format as the original Recorded Exception.
</objective>
