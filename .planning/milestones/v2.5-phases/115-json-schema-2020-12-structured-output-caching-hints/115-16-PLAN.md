<why_the_fence_must_be_structural>
**This is the single most important constraint in this plan set, and the reason a naive closure will
fail verification a fifth time.**

Rounds 1 and 2 were each caught because the defect produced an actual accept-everything bypass —
`(Conforms, Conforms)` where the control gave `(Conforms, Violates)`. **This defect does not.** Both
`dependencies.Inner` and `dependencies.default` enforce `type` identically at `(Violates, Violates)`
on the pinned `jsonschema` 0.49.2 — measured independently by the reviewer (in an isolated crate
pinned to `jsonschema =0.49.2`) and by the verifier (through this crate's own `fuzz_support` seam).

Therefore a behavioural assertion — "the v2 verdict differs", "the pin still rejects the violating
instance" — **would pass against the defective code** and prove nothing. It would be a fence that
cannot fire, which is the exact failure mode this phase has now shipped three times.

The observable that actually distinguishes fixed from broken here is the NORMALIZATION, not the
verdict. Three candidates were considered:

1. **The `Cow` borrow/own decision (`rewritten`) — CHOSEN, together with (2).** It is what was
   measured as flipping (`rewritten=false` vs `true`), and it is what GATES the only D-02 diagnostic:
   `compile_2020_12` warns *only* when `normalize_schema_dialect` returns `Cow::Owned`. Asserting
   `Owned` therefore covers both the normalization and the suppressed warning in one observable, with
   no `tracing` subscriber to install.
2. **The absence of the legacy `$schema` in the normalized bytes at the resource's pointer — CHOSEN.**
   `Owned` alone would be satisfied by a document that was cloned for some *other* declaration, so the
   pointer assertion is what ties the rewrite to the position under test.
3. **The `tracing::warn!` emission — REJECTED as the primary observable.** It is strictly downstream of
   (1) (`compile_2020_12` branches on `matches!(normalized, Cow::Owned(_))`), so it adds no
   discriminating power, and capturing it needs a subscriber harness this module does not have.

This is not a new technique: it is verbatim the one `115-14` used for the `properties`-half of
`v2_pin_still_enforces_an_embedded_resource_named_like_a_data_keyword`
(`src/server/output_validation.rs:1101-1130`), which was written structurally for exactly this reason
and states so in its own comment. That block is the in-repo precedent this task follows.
</why_the_fence_must_be_structural>
