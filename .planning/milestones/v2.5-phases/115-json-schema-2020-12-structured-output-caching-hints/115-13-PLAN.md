<why_not_relax_the_neutrality_predicate>
`115-VERIFICATION.md` `missing:` item 4 offers a choice: relax `is_dialect_neutral` **or** add a
second invariant. Take the second, and here is the measurement that decides it.

`is_dialect_neutral` forbids a nested `$schema` because it is a per-resource dialect switch from
2019-09 onwards and merely ignored in draft-07. After `115-12` that exclusion is MORE necessary, not
less: for a root-2020-12 document with a legacy `$schema` on an embedded resource, v1's auto-detect
honours the switch (vacuous subtree, `Conforms`) while v2 normalizes it away (`Violates`). That is a
LEGITIMATE era divergence in the direction where v2 is stricter — and invariant 3 is an EQUALITY, so
admitting those documents would make the fuzzer fail on correct behaviour.

The correct instrument for those documents is a structural invariant over the NORMALIZED document,
which needs no neutrality reasoning and no skip: invariant 5 below.

The `$ref`/`$defs`/`$id` half of the finding is separable and IS safe to widen — those keywords carry
no dialect switch — so it is done here under one guard: `$ref` is neutral only when it is the SOLE
key of its object, because `$ref` siblings are ignored in draft-07 and honoured under 2020-12 (this
repo already records that as a measured, reachable divergence direction).
</why_not_relax_the_neutrality_predicate>
