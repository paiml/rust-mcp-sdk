<objective>
Make `mcp-tester` reach v2, auto-detect a dual-era server, run the suite twice, and diff the two runs
against the expected-difference baseline — all strictly additively.

Purpose: CLNT-04 plus D-05 and D-06. Auto-detect-then-dual-run was chosen over a
`--protocol-version` flag and over single-era auto-detect because neither can demonstrate that the
two eras AGREE, which is the actual dual-version risk this milestone takes on. A-D11 fixes how: the
strict consumer is a Rust compiler, not a JSON parser — six workspace crates link `mcp_tester`'s
types, and two of those linkages (`TestResult` struct literal at
`cargo-pmcp/src/commands/test/apps.rs:874-878`, exhaustive `TestCategory` match with no `_` arm at
`.../conformance.rs:276-289`) break on exactly the change an always-present comparison section would
want to make.

Output: `--dual-run`, `run_dual`, `DualRunReport`, `ServerTester::with_protocol_version`, and a
live-socket test.
</objective>
