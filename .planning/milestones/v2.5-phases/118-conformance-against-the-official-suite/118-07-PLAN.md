independently; D-17 replaces the impossible fixture route with probes. This plan is where all three

Under v2, Logging is exercised through the `_meta` key `io.modelcontextprotocol/logLevel`, and
Sampling and Roots through `InputRequiredResult` (SEP-2322). The v1 RPC shapes stay green under
**v1 negotiation only**; under v2 the official suite's expectation (removed methods → 404 + -32601)
stands.

## What changed from the pre-review plan

- **No new fixtures, no fixture-format extension.** D-17 closed that option: the grammar supports
  only `tools_list` and a single `tool_call`, so it cannot express a preceding `logging/setLevel`, a
  host-handler installation, a server->client exchange, or an MRTR gather/resend. The
  `contracts/team-servers/fixtures/deprecated-caps/` directory the pre-review plan proposed is NOT
  created, and no plan in this phase creates it.
- **The evidence is probes plus typed completion round trips**, both over the endpoint plan 118-06's
  `spawn_era_target()` binds.
- **`provisional` is applied HERE, by the consumer.** The ported `compare_eras` classifies a
  provisional entry as `Missing` exactly like any other and records `provisional: true` on the row
  (measured in `crates/mcp-tester/src/era_diff.rs` — no exemption exists in the join). This test is
  where the exemption is decided, and after Task 2 there are no provisional rows left anyway.
