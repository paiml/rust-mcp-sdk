## Review corrections applied (D-20)

- **The zero-check policy becomes executable, once.** `conformance/README.md` § 7 (plan 118-02) is
  the normative statement; this plan implements it as `ZERO_CHECK_SCENARIOS` (exact set equality,
  both directions) plus hard-coded `MIN_CHECKS_V1` / `MIN_CHECKS_V2` floors. The values come from
  the measurements plans 118-04 and 118-05 recorded — they are NOT invented here.

  server child alive; the script builds first and execs the binary directly, and its trap kills the
  process group.

  Each suite invocation gets a per-run timeout, and the script gets a total wall-clock budget.
- **Output under `target/conformance-results/`.** `results/` is not gitignored and no plan owns
  `.gitignore`.

  `crates/pmcp-team-servers/tests/era_matrix.rs` behind the non-default `http` feature (D-16), and
  the 33 fixtures stay in `tests/conformance.rs` as the v1 regression guard. `MATRIX_TESTS` covers
  all three, with the right feature flags per target.

  `scripts/lint-plan-verify-commands.sh` (plan 118-10) enforces that for future plans.
