Port the **already-shipped Phase-117 era machinery** from `mcp-tester` into `pmcp-team-servers`
(D-16): the typed observation substrate, the expected-difference baseline model and its total
parser, and the bidirectional `observation_id`-keyed join. Seed the team-servers baseline with one

version rebuilt observations from `CaseResult { case_id, passed, detail }` — which cannot carry a
wire fact, and which emits NOTHING when two eras both pass the same expected response. D-07 already
said *"reuse, do not reinvent"* and Phase 117 already shipped the exact machinery. This plan
transfers it one crate over. **Observations come from EXPLICIT PROBE CODE, never inferred from a
bool.**

total parser, `compare_eras`, `EraComparisonReport`), `baselines/era-deltas.yaml` (14 seeded,

a registered fuzz target.
