## Shared Patterns

### Falsifiable-guard rustdoc (applies to EVERY new test in this phase)

**Source:** `src/shared/sse_parser.rs:862-1004` (both guards) and `:817-845` (the *"this test is NOT the falsifiable complexity guard"* correction block).
**Apply to:** all four new `sse_parser` tests, the over-cap refusal test, and every recorded negative control.

The house rule, stated by CONTEXT `<specifics>`: *"A guard that cannot go RED is not a guard."* The mechanical expression of it in this codebase is:
