is rewritten and that it remains valid for what it booked.

Two things this closure changed are observable to a consumer and must be disclosed rather than left
in a plan summary nobody re-reads:
1. `Transport::receive()` now returns a terminal reason STICKILY. A consumer that loops on `receive()`
   and merely logs errors will spin rather than hang.
2. `Client::dispatch_request` discards a response whose id does not match. Under concurrent calls on
   one client, the request that frame belonged to now waits out its caller's timeout instead of
   receiving a wrong answer. Strictly better, and still a cost.
