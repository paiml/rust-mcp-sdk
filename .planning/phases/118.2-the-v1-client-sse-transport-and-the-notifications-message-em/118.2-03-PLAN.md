<objective>
Apply the incremental reader to the **second** whole-body collect site — the POST response that
answers `text/event-stream` — and retire `SseParser::feed_complete_body`, which loses its last
production caller in the same edit.

Purpose: D-01 widened the roadmap's scope deliberately, and this is the site that made it worth doing.
In StreamableHTTP a POST that answers `text/event-stream` stays open and can carry notifications
**and server-to-client requests** before the response frame. Collecting it whole means progress
arrives only after the call ends, and **in-tool elicitation over a POST stream deadlocks outright** —
the client cannot answer a request it has not parsed yet.

`feed_complete_body`'s disposition is a plan decision, not a build-time discovery (Pitfall 5). After
this edit it has zero non-test callers: under `-D warnings` that is a `dead_code` failure, its own
rustdoc names both call sites as the discharge of its byte-cap precondition and that prose becomes
false, and the tripwire ALLOWLIST justification that cites it goes stale. Deleting is a strict
reduction in attack surface — the rustdoc itself calls a public unbounded parser entry point "an
attractive nuisance."

Output: one reader shape, two sites; `feed_complete_body` gone with all three of its co-located
dependants.
</objective>
