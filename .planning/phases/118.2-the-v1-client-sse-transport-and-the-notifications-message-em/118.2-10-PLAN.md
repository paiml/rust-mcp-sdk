**Pitfall 3 — there is NO client-side notification observation API, and this plan must not invent one.**
`Client.notification_tx: Option<mpsc::Sender<Notification>>` is initialised to `None` at all three
construction sites (`src/client/mod.rs:406`, `:453`, `:496`), `ClientBuilder` has no field for it and no
setter, and the only other reference is the `Clone` impl at `:5263`. The forwarding branch at `:3764` is
therefore permanently dead.

**Decision, made here rather than discovered:** assert at the **transport** level — drive
`StreamableHttpTransport::receive()` directly and match
`TransportMessage::Notification(Notification::Server(ServerNotification::LogMessage(..)))`. Zero new
public API, satisfies "pmcp on both ends" literally, and keeps the `cargo semver-checks` verdict
trivially clean. The `Client`-level observation API (`ClientBuilder::on_notification(..)` or
`Client::subscribe_notifications()`) is additive but is NEW SCOPE this phase did not plan for, and it
would need its own ALWAYS-requirement package (fuzz / property / unit / example). **Record it as a
deferred item; do not build it here.**

`crates/pmcp-team-servers/tests/era_matrix.rs:645-661`:
