<objective>
Close G-5: widen v2 method retirement from the two variants `v2_retired_method_of` matches today
(`resources/subscribe`, `resources/unsubscribe`) to all five methods the 2026-07-28 core schema
retires — `initialize`, `ping`, `logging/setLevel`, `resources/subscribe`, `resources/unsubscribe` —
and key the decision on the METHOD STRING at the ingress rather than on a parsed `Request` variant.

Purpose: the suite sends `params: {_meta: <meta>}` and nothing else. `InitializeParams` requires
`protocolVersion`, `capabilities` and `clientInfo`; `SetLoggingLevel` requires `level`. Both fail
typed parsing, so a variant-based fix would appear to work in unit tests and change nothing on the
wire. Only `ping` currently fails the suite; `initialize` and `logging/setLevel` already pass for the
WRONG reason, through `map_unparsed_body_for_v2`'s documented KNOWN LIMITATION.

Output: a string-keyed ingress predicate, a well-formed-params wire test on both eras, and the
era-matrix G-5 tripwire flipped to the state its own failure message names.
</objective>
