<decision_conflict_surfaced>
A cross-AI review found that nothing in this phase required the conformance legs to actually go
green, so 118.1 could be declared complete with the very gaps it exists to close still red. One
reviewer's remedy was "both legs must report `failed == 0` and exit 0".

That remedy CONFLICTS with three locked decisions and with measured reality, so it is NOT applied
verbatim:
- **D-09** says "widen the gate to what genuinely passes", which presupposes that not everything
  passes.
- **D-21** (carried forward verbatim) scoped the gate precisely BECAUSE the full suite is not green,
  and forbids any suppression that would fake it.
- CONTEXT `<specifics>` names a probable **RFC 9110 OWS-trimming residual** that is NOT one of the
  nine and survives closing G-3, plus two suite checks that "pass for the wrong reason". A
  `failed == 0` exit criterion would therefore be unattainable for reasons that are not this
  phase's defects, and the only ways to satisfy it would be suppression (banned) or abandoning the
  phase.
- The phase boundary in CONTEXT reads "so both era legs move toward exit 0" — move toward, not reach.

**The narrowest reconciliation, applied here and in plan 14:** the exit criterion is scoped to the
gaps rather than to the whole suite, and made total within that scope. `gap_attributable_failures`
must be **0** on both legs, at the held pin (this plan) and at the re-pinned suite (plan 14); every
remaining failure must be classified into exactly one non-gap bucket; and no numbered gap may end
DEFERRED except through plan 12's four-part exit, which only G-3's v2 half may use. This closes the
hole the reviewer found — the phase can no longer complete with its own gaps red — without
overriding D-09, D-21, or the measured residual.
</decision_conflict_surfaced>
