choice and the reason in a comment at the site, and make Task 2's assertion match. Do not leave it
implicit — the whole point of D-09 is that the resolution is VISIBLE, and where it appears is part of

local-file, `pull` is remote-fetch, and `import`/`capture`/`show`/`approve` are platform-side operations
